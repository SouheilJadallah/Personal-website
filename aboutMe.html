About me

A bit more background

I'm Souheil — a Computing student at the University of Guelph. I started programming through personal projects in high school, and what began as curiosity turned into the thing I want to do for a living.

Most of my work lives on the front end: responsive layouts, clean CSS, and JavaScript that adds something instead of getting in the way. My coursework fills in the rest — data structures, databases, software engineering, and enough low-level C to appreciate what the browser does for me.

I like problems that have a visible result. Building a site for a local business, watching a layout finally behave on mobile, refactoring something until it's genuinely simpler — that's the part I keep coming back to.

How I work

Three things I care about

The habits that show up in pretty much everything I build.

✎

Design before code

I sketch the layout and the states first. It's faster to move a box on paper than to untangle CSS later.

🛠

Build it to last

Readable names, small functions, no clever tricks I'd have to re-learn in a month. Future me is the main user.

📚

Keep learning

Every project picks up one new tool or technique on purpose. That's how the list of things I can build keeps growing.
